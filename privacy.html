Privacy Policy

Kingdom Insurance Services, LLC is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you interact with our agency, website, or services.

We may collect the following types of information when you contact us, request a quote, or enroll in insurance services:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Mailing address
  • Type of insurance requested
  • General communication details

We do not collect or request personal medical information on this website.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Providing insurance quotes
  • Responding to inquiries
  • Assisting with enrollment in insurance plans
  • Customer service and communication

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share your information only with:

  • Insurance carriers for enrollment purposes
  • Government agencies when required by law
  • Authorized representatives assisting with your insurance application

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your information
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information when legally permissible

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
